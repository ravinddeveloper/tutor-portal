How Tuition Discovery Works

Whether you are a parent seeking excellence or a dedicated educator, our platform makes pairing seamless.

For Parents & Guardians

1

Submit Your Learning Goals

Specify your student's board (CBSE, ICSE, IB, State), class, weak topics, budget, and preferred days.

2

Smart Tutor Recommendations

Our weighted matching engine calculates location proximity, verified qualification scores, and subject expertise to suggest top candidates.

3

Book a Free Demo Class

Meet the educator in person at your residence or over a live video demo. Evaluate their teaching approach firsthand.

4

Confirm & Track Academic Milestones

Confirm tuition schedules, review regular class notes and attendance, and access monthly student progress reports.

For Tutors & Subject Teachers

1

2

Browse Real-Time Tuition Jobs

View verified student requirements matching your subjects and geographic localities.

3

Unlock Contact or Apply Directly

Use contact credits to access genuine parent phone numbers or submit structured tuition proposals.

4

Schedule Demos & Grow Your Teaching Business

Demonstrate your teaching talent, confirm high-paying monthly engagements, and collect 5-star verified reviews.
