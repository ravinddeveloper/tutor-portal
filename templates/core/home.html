Helpful Clarifications

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about discovering verified educators and getting started

How does the parent-tutor matching process work?
You can either launch our 3-Step Guided Matcher, browse certified educators in your locality directly, or post your requirement for free. Compatible verified teachers apply, and you can arrange a 1-on-1 free trial demo class before committing.
Do parents pay any registration or monthly commission fees?
No, 0% commission. EduTutor Pro is 100% free for parents and students. You settle tuition fees directly with your educator based on agreed hourly or monthly rates with zero middleman markups.
How are tutor degrees and identity credentials verified?
Every educator undergoes rigorous document auditing. We verify Government photo IDs (Aadhaar / Passport) and certified university degree transcripts before awarding the Verified Faculty badge.
What is the free trial demo session?
A complimentary 30 to 45 minute 1-on-1 introductory session conducted either in person at your home or live online. It gives the student and teacher an opportunity to evaluate learning chemistry and syllabus goals before confirming regular classes.
How do educators receive contact credits?
Every newly registered educator automatically receives 3 complimentary welcome credits upon verification to unlock tuition leads. Tutors can later recharge credit packs or subscribe to flexible monthly membership tiers.
