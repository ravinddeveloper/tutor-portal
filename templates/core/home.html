Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about discovering tutors and getting started

How does the parent-tutor matching process work?
When you post your requirements, our platform matches your child's board, class, subjects, and neighborhood with verified tutors who specialize in that area. Compatible tutors apply directly, or you can browse profiles and request a free trial demo session.
Do parents or students pay any platform fees?
No. Posting tuition requirements and discovering verified tutors is 100% free for parents and students. You settle tuition fees directly with your chosen educator based on agreed hourly or monthly rates with zero middleman deductions.
How are tutor profiles verified?
Every educator who registers must provide proof of identity (Government photo ID) and academic degrees (graduation/post-graduation certificates). Our compliance team conducts reviews before granting the Verified Profile badge.
What is the trial demo class?
A trial demo class is a 30 to 45 minute introductory session arranged either in-person at your home or live online. It gives the student and educator a chance to assess rapport, learning style, and teaching methodology before confirming long-term tuition.
How do tutors receive contact credits?
New tutors receive 3 complimentary welcome credits upon registration. You can recharge credit packs or subscribe to monthly membership plans that bundle contact unlocks and prioritized lead matching.
